================================
5. Choosing the Right Collection
================================
Know the exact count, and it won't change?      -> Array        (examScores)
Items get added/removed over time?              -> List<T>      (todoList)
Need to look things up by a unique name/id?     -> Dictionary<K,V> (phoneBook)
